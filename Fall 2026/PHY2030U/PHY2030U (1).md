## 1. General course summary

PHY2030U is an introduction to Newtonian mechanics. The course develops physical intuition, mathematical problem-solving, computational skills, and the ability to communicate solutions clearly.

Main topics:

1. Newton’s laws of motion
2. Cartesian and polar coordinates
3. Air resistance
4. Motion in a magnetic field
5. Momentum and rockets
6. Energy and work
7. Simple harmonic motion
8. Damped and driven oscillations

Required textbook: **Classical Mechanics** by John R. Taylor.

## 2. Course structure

- Two 80-minute lectures each week.
- One 80-minute tutorial most weeks.

## 5. How to solve mechanics problems

1. Describe the physical system.
2. Draw a diagram.
3. Choose coordinates and a sign convention.
4. List knowns and unknowns.
5. Identify the governing principle: Newton’s laws, momentum, energy, or oscillation theory.
6. Write symbolic equations first.
7. Substitute numbers only after the setup is correct.
8. Check units, limiting cases, and whether the result is physically reasonable.
9. Explain the answer clearly.
