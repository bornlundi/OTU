### In-Class Activity: Algorithm to withdrawal money from an ATM
##### My Version: 
Assumptions: 
- Already in front of ATM Machine
- Already have bank card with them in wallet.
- ATM machine already on and does withdrawals.  

1. Read prompt on ATM
2. Get Bank card into hand 
3. Insert bank card
4. Enter your PIN 
5. Locate withdrawal button 
6. Press withdrawal button
7. Press which account to withdraw from
8. Specify amount of money desired
9. Choose your desired bill division
10. Review selection
11. Press Confirm seletion
12. Take cash amount from opening
13. Count the cash yourself to ensure amount is correct.
14. Press yes on panel to confirm you would like a receipt.
15. Press no on panel to confirm you have no more desired actions.
16. Allow machine to return your card to you.
17. Take bank card from machine.
18. Take Receipt from machine. 
19. Place bank card, cash, and receipt in your wallet.
